## Global Coherence: When Every Agent Is Right and the Team Is Still Wrong - A Local-to-Global Semantic Foundation for Multi-Agent Collaboration

https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02036v1

### Claims — model summary, not independent evidence

- The global coherence problem arises when every agent's local actions are valid but the combined result is incorrect.
- The Observation-Aliasing Impossibility Theorem states that a policy can guarantee a valid action from its context if and only if every world consistent with that context allows a common action.

### Methods — model summary

- The paper formulates the global coherence problem and synthesizes relevant limits into specifications for context engineering and agent harnesses.

### Author-reported evidence — not replicated

- In a TeamBench task, a three-agent team overspent a shared 20-call budget in 5/5 runs, but enforcing the budget at commit left 0/5 violations.
- A frontier LLM solves all 40 tasks of a revision benchmark when it can see the deciding event, but when the event is hidden, observed accuracy is compatible with guessing among three options (1/3).

### Limitations — model interpretation

- The framework assumes explicit assumptions on local views, lossless translations, and state updates, and each structure becomes unnecessary when its corresponding map is absent.
- Finding the smallest context that makes validation exact is NP-hard, and greedy selection is essentially the best polynomial-time approximation.

### Claims — model summary, not independent evidence

- The Legal Research Bench (LRB) introduces a benchmark for evaluating the reliability of language model agents in legal research tasks.
- The LRB uses an all-pass grading rubric, where a response is correct only if it satisfies every rubric criterion and every cited authority verifies.

### Methods — model summary

- The LRB includes 413 open-ended U.S. legal research questions authored by experts, each paired with a gold answer, supporting authorities, and a binary grading rubric.
- The evaluation harness allows models to search the web and case law, parse and fetch pages, and recall content into a working database before submitting a final answer.

### Author-reported evidence — not replicated

### Methods — model summary

- We audit all 165 WebArena Lite tasks under six evaluation conditions built from GPT 5.5 and an untrained Qwen3.5 9B model.

### Author-reported evidence — not replicated
