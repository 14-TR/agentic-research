# Locating and Closing Information Gaps in Multi-Agent Systems

https://arxiv.org/html/2610.40186v1

Scientific review with model interpretation. Author-reported results are not independent replication.
No experiments or reproductions were executed by this review workflow.

Coverage: methods, results and limitations checked; tables: checked; figures: checked.

## Author claims

- The paper studies information gaps in fixed multi-agent workflows: evidence can exist in one agent's output yet be absent from the receiver that needs it, either because branches are disconnected or intermediate summaries omit it. (source blocks: H0040, H0033, H0236)
- SPLICE combines selective single-edge repair with a post-trial adopt-or-retain decision conditioned on changes inside the workflow, rather than committing automatically to every modified answer. (source blocks: H0052, H0042, H0072, C0001, C0002)
- The inspected source supports an empirical case for this combined intervention on the evaluated benchmarks, but not a guarantee that each selected edge closes a genuine gap or that every adopted answer improves. (source blocks: T0009, T0025, T0016, T0017, H0040)

## Methods

- The workflow is modeled as a directed acyclic graph whose agents read the query and direct-parent outputs; a repair adds a missing forward edge and reruns only its receiver and affected descendants. (source blocks: H0033, H0042)
- A base-trace score regressor allocates a fixed query budget by predicted headroom, and a separate classifier ranks candidate edges by the probability of changing the final answer, using only pre-trial features at deployment. (source blocks: H0052, H0056, H0060)
- Targeted generation conditions a message on the source answer and entities or numbers mentioned in the receiver's subtask but absent from its parents' outputs; this is an operational missing-item proxy rather than direct observation of the reference-defined gap. (source blocks: H0063, H0040)
- A locally run Qwen selector is trained with supervised fine-tuning followed by DPO on reference-derived beneficial, neutral and harmful labels; deployment uses lexical change, attribution and propagation features and adopts only when p\_b(1−r\_hat)−p\_h r\_hat is positive. (source blocks: H0072, H0079, H0219, T0024, H0099)
- Evaluation uses question-disjoint splits, archived StableToolBench API responses, token F1 for the QA tasks, executable recipe-based PlanCraft scoring, and question-paired bootstrap intervals; ToolBench has 230 held-out requests and the other tasks total 2,142 held-out questions. (source blocks: T0011, H0095, H0227, H0099)

## Author-reported results

- At b=0.5, the authors report ToolBench Micro rising from 0.6347 to 0.6856 on 230 requests, a +0.0509 difference with 95% paired-bootstrap CI \[0.0280,0.0758\], at 1.44× dollar cost; the four-task test-size-weighted mean rises from 0.6654 to 0.7031 on 2,142 questions, +0.0377 \[0.0304,0.0452\], at 1.25× dollar cost. These correspond to approximately 8.0% and 5.7% relative increases, not percentage-point gains of those sizes. (source blocks: T0007, T0009, T0025, H0099, H0095)
- ToolBench ablations report Full 0.6856 versus matched-budget self-revision 0.6689, whose difference from Full is −0.0167 \[−0.0349,−0.0012\], and versus a no-message rerun at 0.6665, −0.0191 \[−0.0375,−0.0112\]; removing process evidence gives 0.6709, −0.0147 \[−0.0334,−0.0071\], while a raw-trace selector gives 0.6772, −0.0084 \[−0.0221,−0.0019\], with 95% question-paired intervals and listed 1.44× cost. (source blocks: T0014, T0009)
- On held-out ToolBench, judge-labeled gap-hit precision@1 is reported as 0.67 \[0.60,0.74\] for SPLICE versus 0.41 \[0.34,0.48\] for random reachable edges; separately, among 120 sampled adopted repairs, both source possession and receiver absence followed by message delivery and revision use jointly hold at rates of 0.74 \[0.66,0.81\] and 0.71 \[0.63,0.79\] under the two judges. (source blocks: T0016, H0126, T0017, H0130)
- For answers to 230 held-out ToolBench questions, the authors' API-support audit reports supported-claim rates of 0.71 \[0.65,0.77\] for Static and 0.83 \[0.78,0.88\] for SPLICE, and unsupported-claim rates of 0.18 \[0.13,0.23\] and 0.09 \[0.06,0.13\]; separately, answer-level completion is 0.63 \[0.57,0.70\] versus 0.74 \[0.68,0.80\]. Claim rates pool claims and average two model judges, whereas completion uses answers and is not Table 1's Served metric. (source blocks: T0018, H0245, T0007, T0009)
- The authors report positive point gains across all six host-workflow-by-task cells, although the 2Wiki-Chain comparison has p=0.0828; selector transfer without retraining on DROP, 2Wiki and PlanCraft yields reported aggregate gains of +0.0502 \[0.026,0.076\] for Kimi-K2.6, +0.0513 \[0.030,0.076\] for DeepSeek-V3.2, +0.0317 \[0.014,0.052\] for Qwen3.7-Max and +0.0284 \[0.012,0.047\] for Qwen3.6-Plus, with the transfer table's population size and task weighting not explicitly specified. (source blocks: T0020, C0012, T0021)
- The inspected figures show higher SPLICE score bars than Static in all nine DROP topology-scale cells, accompanied by additional token cost; the budget figure shows ToolBench peaking at the highlighted half budget while the four-task weighted mean continues improving more slowly, but neither figure displays uncertainty intervals. (source blocks: C0013, C0014)

## Limitations and uncertainty

- Author-stated scope is one gap and one real trial per query, with writing APIs, transactional APIs and multistep repairs deferred to future work; retaining an original answer does not undo tool calls already executed. (source blocks: H0154, H0095)
- Reviewer-inferred statistical limitation: the reported single test evaluation and question-bootstrap intervals do not quantify variability from selector training, workflow sampling or alternate data splits, so the results should not be read as repeated-run robustness estimates. (source blocks: H0227, H0095, H0099)
- Reviewer-inferred mechanism limitation: lexical set overlap and truncated paired texts are imperfect proxies for semantic provenance, and the offline pool preferentially probes two terminal-distance-ranked edges per question; decisive-edge selector statistics therefore do not characterize all candidate edges or deployment queries. (source blocks: H0219, T0024, H0209, H0213)
- Reviewer-inferred audit limitation: model-judge agreement and API matching support the reported grounding assessment but do not establish external factual truth; total audited claim count, human recheck sample size, and the adopted-repair sample's benchmark mixture and budget are not specified in the inspected protocols. (source blocks: H0245, H0130, H0126)
- Reviewer-inferred generalization limitation: backbone transfer is reported only for the named three-task aggregate, its exact population and weighting remain unspecified, and the topology scan reports token costs rather than extending the headline dollar-cost range to every configuration. (source blocks: C0012, T0021, C0013, H0099)

## Critical interpretation

- A substantial strength is the combination of message-content controls, matched-budget self-revision, raw-trace selection and process-feature ablations: together these weaken the explanation that the improvement is merely extra inference, although they do not establish exclusive causal attribution to delivery of the intended fact. (source blocks: T0014, H0219)
- The weighted adoption margin is a useful empirically tested heuristic, not a derived expected-score-gain estimator: multiplying class probabilities by maximum available headroom and loss omits the conditional magnitudes of beneficial and harmful score changes, and its probabilities are not shown to be calibrated by the base-score calibration analysis. (source blocks: H0079, H0072, H0056)
- Output-change prediction should not be equated with gap detection: its supervised target admits irrelevant or harmful changes, while the gap-hit and adopted-repair audits provide partial, judge-dependent corroboration rather than proof that every intervention repairs a structural information gap. (source blocks: H0060, H0040, H0126, T0017)
- The paper should distinguish evidence for the overall selector from evidence for its DPO stage: the SFT-minus-Full interval crosses zero, and a positive 2Wiki-Chain point estimate likewise should not be described as a statistically established host-workflow improvement. (source blocks: T0015, T0020)
- Comparisons with named published methods are best interpreted as comparisons with the authors' specified implementations on shared skeletons and splits, not independent certification of faithful reproduction or uniformly matched compute; cost-performance curves would make that distinction clearer. (source blocks: H0224, H0099, C0014)
- There is a small unresolved reporting inconsistency for the full-budget four-task gain: Table 1 annotates +0.0446 whereas Table 14 reports +0.0447; the inspected half-budget headline is internally aligned, but the conflicting full-budget entries should be reconciled rather than silently corrected. (source blocks: T0009, T0025)
