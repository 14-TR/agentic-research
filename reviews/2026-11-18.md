# PACT: Plan-Action Consistency Training by Preserving Correct Plans and Repairing Actions in GUI Agents

https://arxiv.org/html/2611.22591v1

Scientific review with model interpretation. Author-reported results are not independent replication.
No experiments or reproductions were executed by this review workflow.

Coverage: methods, results and limitations checked; tables: checked; figures: checked.

## Author claims

- The authors identify a plausible credit-assignment conflict in outcome-supervised GUI training: a failed action can give a negative response-level advantage to an otherwise valid immediate plan. (source blocks: H0010, C0003)
- PACT addresses selected, reference-supported execution gaps through local plan-token protection and conditional action-suffix repair, without adding a scalar consistency reward or an inference-time reflection stage. (source blocks: H0052, H0053, H0058, H0060, H0068, H0071, C0005)
- The reported matched comparisons support improved task completion and a favorable repair–retention trade-off for the evaluated checkpoints, but do not establish universal correctness, population-wide mechanism effects, or independent replication. (source blocks: H0211, H0214, T0015, T0029, T0031, H0125)

## Methods

- The mobile selector extracts an explicit immediate plan before reference comparison and applies auxiliary supervision only when the response is judgeable, its plan is reference-supported, its action lacks full credit, and its action is incompatible with the plan; excluded cases remain in base RL. (source blocks: H0052, H0053, H0171, H0173)
- Plan protection clamps negative policy-gradient coefficients to zero only on a localized eligible plan span, leaving positive coefficients, other response tokens, and KL regularization active; failed localization disables protection without necessarily disabling repair. (source blocks: H0058, H0175)
- Action repair teacher-forces a reference-derived positive suffix against the emitted negative suffix under unchanged decoded reasoning, using summed action-token log probabilities and detached pre-update preference weights; its logistic-gradient equivalence is local to the pre-update parameter point. (source blocks: H0060, H0068, H0071)
- The study uses cumulative O, OA, OAR, and PACT configurations from a shared Qwen3.5-9B SFT initialization, with 10,000 mobile and 18,000 desktop RL examples and respective schedules of 1,250 and 562 iterations; repair variants use their own rollouts and retain at most 64 constructible pairs per iteration. (source blocks: T0012, H0084, T0022, H0178)
- Evaluation separates interactive native task success, AndroidControl rule consistency over judgeable outputs, and ScreenSpot point-in-target grounding; AndroidControl retention uses recorded state images with model-specific generated histories rather than fully identical contexts. (source blocks: H0095, H0211, H0214, T0025, H0209, T0027, T0015)
- Mechanism diagnostics separately assess plan correctness, action validity, and semantic consistency using language-model assistance followed by human verification, contrasting fixed-prefix continuation on selected initial states with free generation on a distinct selected-state set. (source blocks: T0029, H0230, T0031, H0232, H0229)

## Author-reported results

- The authors report AndroidWorld success of 80/116 tasks for PACT (68.97%) versus 70/116 for O (60.34%), 71/116 for OA, and 73/116 for OAR; on full OSWorld, PACT achieves 163/369 (44.17%) versus 151/369 for O (40.92%), 152/369 for OA, and 157/369 for OAR, with success requiring native score 1.0 and no reported uncertainty intervals. (source blocks: H0211, H0214)
- On AndroidControl High, reported rule consistency rises from O's 6,879/8,218 judgeable outputs (83.71%) to PACT's 7,046/7,852 (89.74%); on Low, PACT's 7,155/8,172 (87.56%) exceeds O's 7,182/8,313 (86.39%) as a ratio but not as an absolute consistent-output count, and remains below OAR's 7,270/8,248 (88.14%). (source blocks: T0025, H0209)
- ScreenSpot-v2 Mobile grounding is reported as 481/501 hits for PACT (96.01%), tied with OA and above O's 460/501 (91.82%) and OAR's 478/501 (95.41%); PACT retains 459/460 O-correct targets (99.78%), compared with OA's 458/460 and OAR's 456/460. (source blocks: T0027, T0028, C0009)
- On aligned AndroidControl images, PACT's reported O-correct action retention is 89.51% on High and 92.37% on Low, versus OAR's 86.34% and 89.07%, yielding 207 and 313 additional reference matches over OAR; nevertheless, Low PACT has 502 regressions versus 476 repairs and therefore 26 fewer matches than O. (source blocks: T0015, H0095, C0009)
- On the selected 23-state initial-step diagnostic, fresh fixed-plan continuation yields 23/23 reference matches for PACT and OAR, 22/23 for OA, and 13/23 for O; free-generation reference matching is 22/23 for every repair variant, while semantic joint repair is 23/23 for OA and 22/23 for PACT and OAR, despite all three being semantically consistent on 23/23. (source blocks: T0029, H0230, C0023)
- On the distinct 30-state O-selected natural-gap diagnostic, the authors report PACT versus OAR plan correctness of 23/30 versus 19/30, action validity and joint repair of 19/30 versus 13/30, and consistency of 28/30 versus 25/30; among the 24 states consistent for both models, joint repair is 16/24 versus 12/24, without reported uncertainty intervals. (source blocks: T0031, H0232, H0229)

## Limitations and uncertainty

- The authors explicitly acknowledge that requiring explicit plans verifiable against a single reference excludes some implicit valid plans and semantically equivalent alternative actions. (source blocks: H0125)
- The authors explicitly acknowledge that desktop evidence is task-level, whereas the fine-grained plan and fixed-plan diagnostics are confined to mobile states. (source blocks: H0125)
- Reviewer-identified uncertainty remains about robustness across training seeds and environment realizations: the inspected results provide checkpoint-level point estimates without confidence intervals or repeated-run dispersion, so statistical significance and reproducibility are not established. (source blocks: T0022, H0211, H0214, T0029, T0031)
- Reviewer-identified implementation uncertainties include unreported selector precision and coverage, localization-failure frequency, effective protected-token and repair-pair counts, and incomplete SFT data provenance; the authors' benchmark-disjointness assertion has not been independently audited. (source blocks: H0053, H0190, H0175, H0178)
- Reviewer-identified diagnostic limitations include baseline-selected small samples and incompletely specified annotation reliability; language-model assistance plus human verification does not by itself establish blinded, independently reproducible semantic judgments. (source blocks: T0029, H0230, H0232, H0229)

## Critical interpretation

- The cumulative ablations are a useful strength, particularly the direct OAR-to-PACT comparison, but they do not fully separate plan protection from changes in on-policy data or effective auxiliary-supervision dose; a protection-only condition and matched-pair exposure controls would strengthen causal attribution. (source blocks: T0012, H0084, H0175, H0178)
- Rule consistency should not be presented as action correctness: model-dependent exclusion rates alter its denominator, and the Low setting demonstrates that a higher ratio can coexist with fewer absolute consistent outputs; reporting all-state and common-judgeable-set results alongside semantic audits would improve interpretation. (source blocks: T0025, H0209, C0020, H0256)
- The fixed-plan intervention usefully holds decoded reasoning constant across models, but the selected launch-error population limits generalization, and O's improvement under fresh continuation shows that prefix continuation itself changes the evaluation condition; these results support conditional action-generation gains rather than proving that the stated plan causally mediates ordinary end-to-end behavior. (source blocks: T0029, H0230, C0023)
- AndroidControl retention aligns recorded images but allows different generated histories, so it mixes local action preservation with history effects; the history-independent ScreenSpot result is valuable corroboration but remains a static grounding test, not evidence that retention mediates interactive task gains. (source blocks: H0095, T0028, T0015)
- Reference-derived spatial positives are not guaranteed to reproduce the exact stated coordinate because reference support uses a broader tolerance than plan–action compatibility; semantic target checks or explicit reporting of positive-suffix compatibility would test whether the auxiliary pairs always instantiate the intended alignment. (source blocks: H0171, H0173, H0177)
- The defensible comparative conclusion is superiority within the matched platform-specific experiments, not a controlled state-of-the-art ranking across published agents; some external desktop entries use OSWorld-Verified, and illustrative success panels do not substitute for independent execution or file-content verification. (source blocks: H0211, H0214, C0021, C0022)
