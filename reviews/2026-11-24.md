# From Local Errors to System Failures: Modeling and Benchmarking Error Propagation in Multi-Agent LLM Systems

https://arxiv.org/html/2611.31063v1

Scientific review with model interpretation. Author-reported results are not independent replication.
No experiments or reproductions were executed by this review workflow.

Coverage: methods, results and limitations checked; tables: checked; figures: checked.

## Author claims

- REPA's central contribution is a fitted local-to-endpoint model that separates input assembly from agent processing and composes their nonnegative response maps over a finite invocation graph. (source blocks: H0026, H0034, H0170, H0045, C0001)
- Once fitted, the forward model predicts observer-defined content discrepancies from paired source measurements and execution structure without downstream test observations or task-model fine-tuning; its susceptibility scores are not task-failure probabilities. (source blocks: H0029, H0030, H0048)
- The reported prediction and controlled-intervention studies support further investigation of local response modeling, but they do not establish causal identification of semantic-error transmission or generally optimal recovery policies. (source blocks: H0034, T0009, H0258, H0263, H0265, H0300)

## Methods

- Reference and perturbed executions are paired under matched configurations, with each invocation represented as a node and finite feedback unfolded into a DAG; common exposure perturbs shared inputs, whereas localized exposure targets one recipient. (source blocks: H0026, H0062)
- Input-assembly and processing maps are fitted separately by nonnegative ridge regression with penalty 0.1, no intercept and no feature standardization, then frozen for recursive evaluation using predicted parent outputs. (source blocks: H0030, H0170, H0062)
- The finite path-sum construction includes source placement and terminal processing, while the residual analysis bounds endpoint error conditional on local residual bounds and fixed fitted coefficients. (source blocks: H0045, H0205, H0209)
- ErrPropBench-10 combines seven knowledge settings with three multi-hop QA settings, with 100 fitting, 20 validation and 30 test questions per setting; fact-bundle grouping prevents related authored questions from crossing splits, but these roster counts do not determine every analysis denominator. (source blocks: H0058, H0233, H0234)
- The observer audit evaluates signed severity changes on 200 paired HotpotQA instances against a two-model, two-prompt reference using Lin's CCC; the archived forward predictor instead uses a distinct direct-discrepancy protocol that was not fully label-blind. (source blocks: H0068, H0244, H0069, H0070, H0286)
- The inverse diagnostic fits a separate scalar model of missing role content, restoration policies can reinstate one Healthy prompt, and clean-input RAG strategies reuse a question-specific BM25/MMR evidence packet while varying delivery sites. (source blocks: H0258, H0262, H0263, H0265)

## Author-reported results

- In the authors' HotpotQA common-exposure comparison, Q14 mesh accuracy decreases from 0.38 to 0.08 and feedback accuracy from 0.34 to 0.28; the inspected plots support workflow-dependent susceptibility, but provide no uncertainty estimates for these accuracy differences. (source blocks: H0076)
- Table 1 reports condition-level Spearman correlations of 0.84, 0.85 and 0.83 for REPA on Q14, Q9 and Q122, versus 0.58, 0.46 and 0.36 for direct-matrix regression; endpoint-vector MAEs are respectively 0.24, 0.23 and 0.22 versus 0.26, 0.25 and 0.25, without reported intervals or effective evaluation counts. (source blocks: T0009, H0079, H0252)
- In the separate Q14 artificial-disablement study, Original REPA is plotted at localization success 0.77 and FPR 0.05 versus local differences at 0.43 and 0.23, with 95% confidence bars; the restoration study displays REPA accuracy midpoint 0.31 versus Disabled 0.17 and Healthy 0.32, with bounds whose statistical interpretation is unspecified. (source blocks: H0080, H0258, H0086, C0006)
- For the plotted clean-input HotpotQA RAG comparison, REPA-RAG versus None accuracy is 0.55 versus 0.31 on Q9, 0.49 versus 0.33 on Q14 and 0.67 versus 0.51 on Q122; these gains average approximately 19 percentage points across the three displayed models, while the authors report 16% additional tokens and the plot supplies relative-cost curves rather than raw token totals or uncertainty intervals. (source blocks: C0006, H0265, H0003)
- Across ten ErrPropBench-10 settings, the authors report macro-average Spearman correlation 0.81 and MAE 0.29; DNA's 0.93 correlation and 0.02 MAE contrast with Photosynthesis's 0.82 and 0.39, illustrating that good propagation ranking does not guarantee accurate magnitudes. (source blocks: H0273, C0008)
- The sample-count figure shows decreasing normalized variability across six Q14 roles, but the measured statistic is a resampled mean sentence-overlap distance ratio rather than uncertainty in the fitted semantic response matrices or held-out endpoint predictions. (source blocks: H0267, H0269, C0007)

## Limitations and uncertainty

- The authors explicitly limit demonstrated generalization to three Qwen checkpoints and six workflows, leaving other model families, heterogeneous agents and adaptive tool-using execution structures open. (source blocks: H0300)
- The authors identify bounded domain coverage as a limitation, and the benchmark's partly model-authored knowledge questions do not by themselves establish performance on broader naturalistic tasks. (source blocks: H0233, H0301)
- Observer agreement is measured against a model-based reference rather than established human ground truth, and the signed-severity audit does not validate the legacy discrepancy labels used for forward fitting and evaluation. (source blocks: H0068, H0244, H0069, H0070, H0286)
- The inspected source does not provide effective eligible-pair and condition counts for the main prediction estimates, fully operational normalization details, exact localization interval endpoints, a definition of the restoration bounds, or raw RAG token totals; the retained numerical conclusions are therefore descriptive author-reported estimates rather than significance or precision claims. (source blocks: H0048, H0234, T0009, H0080, H0086, C0006, H0003)
- The fixed-fit residual identities do not establish that residuals are small or quantify refitting uncertainty, and inverse recovery relies on paired Healthy traces and artificial missing-content faults rather than demonstrated diagnosis of unrestricted factual failures. (source blocks: H0205, H0209, H0258, H0262, H0263)
- Cross-model results are configuration comparisons rather than isolated model-size effects, and the unexplained Q9 deployment description of two GPUs with TP4 remains a reproducibility uncertainty rather than a setting corrected by this review. (source blocks: T0030, H0237)

## Critical interpretation

- The separation of assembly and processing is interpretable, but REPA's fixed-configuration endpoint map is still linear in the source vector and uses additional intermediate supervision during fitting; its advantage over direct regression therefore does not isolate topology composition from supervision, parameterization and regularization effects. (source blocks: H0030, H0034, H0170, H0045, T0009, H0252)
- The most consequential validity issue is protocol mismatch: the well-described severity-change audit is not the measurement protocol behind the main forward results, and historical access to injection metadata leaves a plausible label-cue bias that agreement among reference models cannot exclude. (source blocks: H0068, H0244, H0070, H0286)
- The RAG study usefully evaluates an accuracy–cost trade-off with shared evidence, but Global delivers more copies and Entry can address multiple roots, so it does not isolate site selection at equal evidence or token budgets; the plotted Q14 accuracy deficit relative to Global also rules out uniform accuracy dominance. (source blocks: C0006, H0265, H0087)
- The one-node restoration budget makes repair-location policies comparable in a narrow sense, but paired Healthy traces, placeholder faults and offline candidate reruns prevent interpreting the study as an end-to-end low-cost solution to naturally occurring failures. (source blocks: H0258, H0262, H0263, H0086)
- The stability analysis motivates collection effort only for its sentence-overlap proxy, not for semantic-matrix convergence; zero input-distance handling is unspecified, and the text's Fig. 7 references for this diagnostic conflict with the actual Figure 6 caption. (source blocks: H0267, H0269, C0007)
- The authors reasonably explain why prior systems were not direct baselines, but the resulting evidence supports comparisons with the supplied regression and repair controls rather than broad empirical superiority over external diagnosis, propagation or recovery methods. (source blocks: H0227, H0252)
